What is the SAMA Cyber Security Framework?

Version 1.0 · May 2017

  • Issued by the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) for the organizations it regulates (Member Organizations).
  • Principle-based (2.2): SAMA sets principles and objectives, and lists control considerations to achieve them. If a control consideration cannot be implemented, the organization applies compensating controls, accepts the risk internally and requests a formal waiver from SAMA.
  • Self-assessment (2.3): the organization assesses itself with a questionnaire, and SAMA reviews and audits the result to determine compliance and maturity.
  • Maturity, not pass/fail (2.4): each subdomain gets a maturity level from 0 to 5. The minimum required is level 3.

Structure: 4 domains, 32 subdomains

Each subdomain = Principle → Objective → Control considerations

Level 4 adds KRIs with thresholds and effectiveness measurement (2.4.2). Level 5 adds continuous improvement, integration with enterprise risk management and comparison with peer and sector data (2.4.3).

How this tool assesses

  1. Controls & Evidence: read what SAMA requires and the evidence needed, to support each Yes / No answer.
  2. Assessment: answer the 14 maturity criteria (taken from Table 1 and section 2.4) with Yes or No for each subdomain.
  3. The level is the highest level whose criteria, and all lower ones, are Yes. No averaging.
  4. Every unmet criterion up to level 3 becomes an action in the Action Plan.
  5. A subdomain is Not assessed until all 14 criteria are answered; it is not counted as level 0.
  6. All dates (overdue, escalation, due in 30 days) are calculated from one assessment date (as of date).

Sources: SAMA Rulebook - Cyber Security Framework · official framework document v1.0 (May 2017).

Action Plan to Level 3

Generated automatically from the assessment: every criterion up to level 3 answered No becomes an action. Closing an action does not change the answer or the level: the criterion stays No until it is re-assessed with evidence in the Assessment. Closed actions are flagged Re-assess. Risk accepted actions stay unmet; their target date is the acceptance expiry date.
